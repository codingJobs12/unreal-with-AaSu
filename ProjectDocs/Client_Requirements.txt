STORY

The player is a powerful mage whose magical abilities were stolen by Isabel, a possessed doll.

The player explores the forest and recovers powers by collecting magical coins. Coins act as currency and are used to purchase stolen abilities through upgrade stations.

The player completes objectives, defeats enemies, collects coins and eventually fights Isabel.

TEDDY BEAR COMPANION

Timmy is a magical teddy bear who:

- Explains the story
- Gives hints and objectives
- Explains gameplay mechanics
- Guides the player to the next objective
- Helps unlock abilities
